# Warranty

Every new bike from Harbour E-Bikes has this warranty:

- Frame: 5 years
- Motor and electronics: 2 years
- Battery: 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first

Not covered: wear parts (tyres, tubes, brake pads, chain, cassette), damage from accidents, and bikes changed or opened by someone other than an authorised workshop. You need your proof of purchase.
