# Booking and cancelling a rental

- Book online at https://harbour-ebikes.example/rent or in the shop.
- Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start of the rental.
- Cancellation less than 24 hours before the start: we charge 50% of the rental price.
- If you do not come and do not cancel, we charge the full rental price.
