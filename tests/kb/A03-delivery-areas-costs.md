# Delivery areas and costs

**Bikes** are delivered fully assembled, only in these areas:

| Area | Delivery cost |
|---|---|
| Portwell and Eastbay | free for orders over €500, otherwise €25 |
| Kelmara Island (by ferry) | €45 for every order |

We do not deliver bikes outside these areas. You can collect your bike at the shop for free.

**Parts and accessories** are sent by post to any address in the country: €4.95, free for orders over €60. We do not ship abroad.
