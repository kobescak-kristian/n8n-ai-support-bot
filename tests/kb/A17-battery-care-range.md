# Battery care and range

- For daily use, keep the charge between 20% and 80%.
- Store the battery at 10 to 25 °C. Do not leave it in a hot car or in the sun.
- If you do not ride for a long time, charge it to about 60% every 3 months.
- A full charge takes about 4 to 6 hours.
- Range on one charge: about 60 to 100 km, depending on the model, the assist level, the weight and the hills.
