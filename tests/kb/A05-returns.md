# Returns and refunds

This policy applies to all orders from 1 June 2026.

- You can return a new bike, part or accessory within **30 days** of delivery.
- The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
- To return a bike, we collect it from you. The pickup fee is €35. Parts and accessories you can send back by post at your own cost, or bring to the shop for free.
- Not returnable: used bikes, custom builds and gift cards.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 working days after we receive the item.

Start a return by e-mail to support@harbour-ebikes.example with your order number.
