# How to make a warranty claim

1. E-mail support@harbour-ebikes.example with your order number, a short description and photos of the problem.
2. We reply within 2 working days and tell you whether to bring the bike to the shop.
3. Warranty repairs are free, including the diagnostic check.

Never open the battery yourself: an opened battery loses its warranty.
