# Booking a repair or service

- Book a service slot at https://harbour-ebikes.example/service or by phone on 0100 555 0142.
- Standard service: €69. It includes brakes, gears, a check of the motor and a battery test.
- The usual waiting time for a slot is 3 to 5 working days.
- We also repair e-bikes bought elsewhere.
