# Where we are and parking

Harbour E-Bikes, Harbour Road 12, Portwell.

- The shop is a 5-minute walk from the Portwell ferry terminal.
- There are 2 free customer parking spaces behind the shop. Enter from Mill Lane.
- Bus lines 3 and 7 stop at "Harbour Road", in front of the shop.
