# Battery safety: smoke, heat or swelling

If the battery smokes, smells of burning, swells, leaks or is very hot:

1. Stop riding or charging at once and unplug the charger if it is safe.
2. Move away from the battery and keep other people away. Do not touch it if it is hot.
3. If there is fire or heavy smoke, call the emergency number 112.
4. Do not use the battery again. Contact us so a colleague can help you.

If you are injured in an accident, call 112 first.
