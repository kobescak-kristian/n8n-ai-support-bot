# Discounts and prices

- Students get 10% off accessories (not bikes, rentals or repairs) with a valid student card, in the shop only.
- Newsletter subscribers get free postage on parts and accessories.
- We have no other discount codes. Prices are fixed and not negotiable.
- The chat cannot give discounts, refunds or exceptions.
