# Test rides

- Test rides are free and last 30 minutes, during opening hours.
- Bring a photo ID.
- No booking needed for Harbour City and Harbour Trail. For Harbour Cargo, please book a test ride by phone on 0100 555 0142, because we have only one test bike.
