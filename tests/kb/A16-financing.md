# Paying in instalments

- Bikes from €1,000 can be paid in 12 monthly instalments at 0% interest.
- Instalments are offered by our finance partner, who does a credit check.
- Instalments are not available for rentals, repairs, parts or gift cards.
- Apply in the shop or at checkout online.
