# Delivery times and tracking

- Bikes in stock: delivered within 2 to 4 working days after payment.
- Kelmara Island: 3 to 5 working days, because of the ferry schedule.
- Parts and accessories: posted within 1 working day.

When your order leaves us, you get an e-mail with a tracking link. You can also track your order at https://harbour-ebikes.example/track with your order number and postcode.
