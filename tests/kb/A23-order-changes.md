# Changing or cancelling an order

- You can change or cancel an order free of charge until it is sent. E-mail support@harbour-ebikes.example with your order number.
- Once the order is sent, cancel it with a return (see "Returns and refunds").
- This chat cannot see your order, payment or refund status. Ask for a colleague and they will check it for you.
