# Rental deposit, ID and age

- Every rental needs a deposit of €200, held on a credit or debit card. We do not take cash deposits.
- The deposit is released within 3 working days after you return the bike.
- The deposit cannot be waived or reduced.
- Bring a photo ID. Minimum age is 16; riders aged 16 or 17 need a parent's signature.
